There is an intoxicating felling of control that comes from knowing there is no safety and you 
can easily shoot yourself in the foot. 

Strucutres evolve by building in defence mechanisms: chains of responsibility, review processes. permission
management to make sure one person's bad bash variable resolution doesn't nuke an entire production database.
The accompanying costs are beared not only in weight of the processes, but more suttly by the pernitious nature
of exactly what they are designed to accomplish: dilluting responsability. 

In physiques, the stable state is that of lowest energy, this methaphore is applicable to the human mind. 
Our default, most comfortable state is that of a calm, low calorie expenditure, sparing ourselfs the expese
of thinking mayatically though every minute corner case. 

It is no wonder, that one fells comfort being part of a structured organization where individual misssteps won't
cause the collapse of entire projects. The only failure mode is collective, dilluting the sense of responsability. 

Predictably, the mind let's itself spare the expense of anlysing every contrived interaction, the author spares
himself the undirected responsibility of checking every corner case, why would he if it isn't his job? 
And so, areas learned to be left partialy missunderstood and the mind is never forced to sharpen to it's full paranoiac potenciel.
