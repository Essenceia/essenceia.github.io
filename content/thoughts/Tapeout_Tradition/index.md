## The Dress Code

As you have already well understood dead reader this trip down to Waffle House isn't a casual affrair but part of a tradition. 
And accomanying it's status as tradition it comes with a dress code. 

Although most partrons of this estemeed establishement might find it perfectly acceptable to come adorned in there
finest morning pygamas completed with pink bunny stippers this is not the dress code our outing requires. 

Rather, the tapeout tradition requires of you to disregard your inability to walk straight, and make the exrutiating trip up 
the stairs and into your dressing because this dress code is formal. 

More specifically, since refering to "formal dress" is somewhat inprecise, we are going for the "one star michelin restaurant" formal which on the is localed 
slightly above "interview dress" and bellow "I could get married in this".
For men this means polished leather shoes, neat dress pants with matching shirt, ties and vests optional. 
For women this means elegant heals, mid length or long dresses and spending additional time before heading out in an attempt to hide the decheveled traces of the previous all nighter. 

Do not mind the confused looks of the staff and patrons as you step inside the establishement but fret not, as you are not here simply for a heartwarming 4pm breakfast but as a celebration.
