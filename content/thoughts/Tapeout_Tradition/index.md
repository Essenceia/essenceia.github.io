## The Dress Code

As you have already well understood dead reader this trip down to Waffle House isn't a casual affrair but part of a tradition. 
And accomanying it's status as tradition it comes with a dress code. 

Although most partrons of this estemeed establishement might find it perfectly acceptable to come adorned in there
finest morning pygamas completed with pink bunny stippers this is not the dress code our outing requires. 

Rather, the tapeout tradition requires of you to disregard your inability to walk straight, and make the exrutiating trip up 
the stairs and into your dressing because this dress code is formal. 

More specifically, since refering to "formal dress" is somewhat inprecise, we are going for the "one star michelin restaurant" formal which on the is localed 
slightly above "interview dress" and bellow "I could get married in this".
For men this means polished leather shoes, neat dress pants with matching shirt, ties and vests optional. 
For women this means elegant heals, mid length or long dresses and spending additional time before heading out in an attempt to hide the decheveled traces of the previous all nighter. 

Do not mind the confused looks of the staff and patrons as you step inside the establishement but fret not, as you are not here simply for a heartwarming 4pm breakfast but as a celebration. 

## The Menu

One brewed coffee and a plate of waffles, cooked to taste.

Yet our tradition does not call upon coffee's for it's taste or the refinement of it's aroma, it's inclusion onto our menu is a matter of cold practicality. 
We come not in search of coffee, we come in need of cafeine, and in this quest the `2.5 $` unlimited hot brew is the optimimum solution.

The choice of the main dish shall come as no suprise to be the specialty of the house: a plate of freshly cooked waffles. 
At this point I would like to warn patrons unfamiliar with Waffle House about one of the perticularities of this establishement: assuming you come outside of rush hours, 
your waiter cookes will do there
upmost to satisfy your request, regardless of if your mostrous creation is on the menu. So before coffee comes to rescue your clouded mind be mindfull of your order
else you "waffle with bacon" may end up as precisely what you requested.

The predefined choice of menu is not arbirary but rather serves a defined triple purpose. 
Firstly there is the visual symbolism of the waffle which, dear reader, I believe needs no further explaination.
Secondly this pre-set menu protects sleep deprived goes from the worst of there culinary exuberances.
Lastly, it removes choice, which after having battled for days on end making choice upon choice, is unikely liberating. 

## All that is left

As your plate of warm waffles is served you take a bite and slowly sink into the stall, for it is done. 
The deadline has been meet, the chip has been sent off to the fab, and as the exileration the was keeping you 
going washes away it is time for a silent celebration.
