The future doesn't exist. This place towards which I waisted today walking is but a fleeting mirage, always on the horizon.

Tomorrow will be tomorrow's today, and today will be tomorrow's yesterday.
Only today and my memories of the past exist, the future is but a conjuring of the mind, a fantasy in which the present moment might drown.

Fixated on the future, I can forget to live today, and then nothing exists anymore.

So if I want to live, I can only live for today, prepare tomorrow and stop chasing shadows of the future.
